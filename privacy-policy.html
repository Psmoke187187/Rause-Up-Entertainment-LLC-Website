Privacy Policy

Raise Up Entertainment LLC — last updated November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Raise Up Entertainment LLC ("Raise Up Entertainment," "we," "us") handles information in connection with the Raise Up Entertainment mobile app and the website raiseupentertainmentllc.com (together, the "Service").

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal data through the app. The Raise Up Entertainment app does not require an account, does not ask you to sign in, and does not collect, store, or share your personal information.

If you contact us directly — for example, by email to book a studio session, buy a beat, or ask about event production — we receive the information you choose to send us (such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message) and use it only to respond to you.

Payments

Purchases made through links in the Service (such as beats or apparel) are processed by third-party payment and storefront providers (such as Stripe and Printful). Your payment information is handled by those providers under their own privacy policies. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

Third-Party Services

The Service may link to third-party websites and platforms (for example, streaming services and social media). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties, and we encourage you to review their policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Because the app does not collect personal data, there is no app-collected data to share with third parties.

Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for a general audience aged 18 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.
