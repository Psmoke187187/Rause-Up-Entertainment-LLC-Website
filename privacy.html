Your Privacy Choices

Enjoy the music.
Choose your privacy.

Optional analytics helps Raise Up Entertainment LLC understand how visitors use this website. You can decline analytics and still listen to previews, browse products, and purchase beats.

What optional analytics measures

With your permission, we record page visits, active visible time, beat previews and listening time, genre filters, the number of search results, product interest, contact clicks, and checkout-link clicks. We also record the referring website or campaign label and a broad device category.

Random browser and visit identifiers help estimate repeat visits. A new visit begins after 30 minutes of inactivity. These identifiers do not identify you by name. Declining analytics removes our analytics identifiers and stops future activity collection from this browser.

What we leave out

We do not put names, email addresses, payment information, raw typed search text, or recordings of your screen into activity reports. We do not store raw IP addresses in our activity database. Hosting and payment providers may process technical information when serving requests under their own policies.

Private reports and payments

Activity reports are available only in the business owner's private dashboard. Reports cover up to 90 days of activity; older activity is pruned as new events arrive. Verified Stripe payment and refund records are used for sales reporting, including when analytics is declined. Payment processing is separate from optional browsing analytics.

Your control

We remember your analytics choice for up to 180 days. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals keep optional analytics off. You can change your choice at any time using Analytics preferences below. To restore a browser previously excluded from owner reports, use Restore analytics choice.
