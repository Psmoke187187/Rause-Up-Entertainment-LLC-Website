Your Privacy Choices

Enjoy the music.
Choose your privacy.

This policy applies to the Raise Up Entertainment LLC website and Android app (com.raiseupentertainment.app). Raise Up Entertainment LLC is responsible for the activity and order information described here. Privacy contact: raiseupentllc@gmail.com.

Optional analytics helps us understand how visitors use the website and app. You can decline analytics and still listen to previews, browse products, and purchase beats and sound packs. Browsing does not require a customer account.

What optional analytics measures

With your permission, we record page visits, active visible time, beat previews and listening time, genre filters, the number of search results, product interest, contact clicks, and checkout-link clicks. We also record the referring website or campaign label and a broad device category.

Random browser and visit identifiers help estimate repeat visits. A new visit begins after 30 minutes of inactivity. These identifiers do not identify you by name. Declining analytics removes our analytics identifiers and stops future activity collection from this browser.

What we leave out

We do not put names, email addresses, payment information, raw typed search text, or recordings of your screen into activity reports. We do not store raw IP addresses in our activity database. Hosting and payment providers may process technical information when serving requests under their own policies.

Orders, delivery, and support

When you choose to purchase, we receive order information from the payment service, including your name and email address when supplied, the products purchased, payment status, and refund information. We use this information to fulfill orders, send purchase and download information, provide support, and keep purchase records. Purchase history is also used for private sales reporting. You can browse and listen to previews without placing an order or supplying order contact details.

Stripe collects payment details directly under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive full card numbers. With optional analytics allowed, a random visit identifier may be included with checkout to connect a purchase to the corresponding visit in private reports. Order and support records are retained as needed to fulfill purchases, provide support, and meet applicable recordkeeping requirements.

Service providers and security

We use hosting and analytics infrastructure, Stripe for payment processing, and Google email and Drive services for order communication and file delivery. These services process information needed to provide their services. Activity reporting does not sell personal information or provide advertising profiles to other businesses. Website, app, analytics, and checkout connections use HTTPS to protect data in transit.

Private reports and payments

Activity reports are available only in the business owner's private dashboard. Reports cover up to 90 days of activity; older activity is pruned as new events arrive. Verified Stripe payment and refund records are used for sales reporting, including when analytics is declined. Payment processing is separate from optional browsing analytics.

Your control

We remember your analytics choice for up to 180 days. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals keep optional analytics off. You can change your choice at any time using Analytics preferences below. To restore a browser previously excluded from owner reports, use Restore analytics choice.
