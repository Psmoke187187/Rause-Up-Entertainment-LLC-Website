Licensee must obtain a written upgrade before exceeding a limit or pause the affected use at the limit. Licensee may edit the beat for the song and authorize service providers, distributors and platforms to act for those permitted uses. No standalone beat or stem distribution, resale, sample-pack use, or transfer of this license without written consent.

3. Beat ownership and prior rights. Licensor retains ownership of the underlying beat and beat recording and may license the beat to others. A later exclusive deal does not cancel this license while Licensee complies with its terms.

4. Master, publishing and credit. Licensee owns the new song master subject to this license. Unless a signed split sheet states otherwise, the beat contributors collectively hold 50% of the new song composition and Licensee’s songwriters collectively hold 50%. Confirm each contributor’s legal name, share and publisher before release. Each side collects its own share. No additional producer royalty on the new master is due under this license. Credit “Produced by Raise Up Entertainment” in release metadata and video descriptions where supported.

5. Rights and automated claims. Licensee may not enroll the beat or new song in Content ID or similar automated rights-claim systems. Licensee must disable distributor opt-ins and promptly release claims caused by its use. Licensor represents it has authority to grant these rights subject to the written disclosures. Licensee is responsible for clearing its additions. Any disclosed third-party restrictions must be resolved in writing before release.

6. Breach and governing law. Either party may give written notice of material breach. Licensor may terminate the grant if Licensee fails to cure within 15 days after receipt. On termination, Licensee must stop covered uses and request takedowns. Pennsylvania law governs, subject to applicable federal law. Changes require written agreement.
